# Product

## Register

product

## Users

RepFast is for strength trainees who already know what they are lifting and need a reliable log during a real gym session. The primary user is between working sets, often one-handed, fatigued, in low light, and dealing with unreliable network access.

## Product Purpose

RepFast exists to make strength logging faster than paper while giving the immediate insight paper cannot: what changed since last time. The first offline MVP should open directly into an active workout, load previous set defaults, save the current set locally, start rest timing, and show a compact comparison without forcing navigation.

Success means a lifter can log a set in under 10 seconds, trust the log to work without signal, and know whether the current set improved, matched, or regressed versus the last comparable session.

## Brand Personality

Fast, focused, hard-wearing.

RepFast should feel like a serious gym instrument, not a wellness feed. The voice is terse and practical. It should reward progress clearly without motivational clutter.

## Anti-references

- Social fitness feeds, reactions, follower counts, and public sharing.
- Coaching clutter, video lessons, meal planning, habit gamification, or broad wellness dashboards.
- Spreadsheet-like tables during the active workout.
- Decorative dark-mode UI that slows recognition or reduces contrast.
- Signup-first or cloud-required flows.

## Design Principles

1. The log loop is the product. Every screen must protect the under-10-second set entry promise.
2. Offline is normal. Local persistence is a core behavior, not a fallback warning state.
3. Previous values should do the work. The app should remember and prefill what the lifter most likely needs.
4. Compare immediately. The emotional payoff is a plain answer about what changed since last time.
5. Keep power features behind the MVP. Settings, kg/lb preferences, plate calculator, RPE, sync, wearables, and voice can come later.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The MVP targets WCAG AA contrast where applicable and mobile touch targets of at least 44px. It must support reduced motion, avoid color-only meaning, use readable labels for screen readers, and keep the active logging flow usable with shaky hands and one thumb.
